# Lab 02 — Windows Incident Triage

Scenario: FILE-01 becomes unavailable after suspicious activity begins on WIN-FIN-07.

Student mission:
- Identify the earliest confirmed suspicious execution.
- Correlate endpoint activity with authentication events.
- Determine when privileged identity use becomes anomalous.
- Identify the first affected critical server.
- Establish the outage window.

Success condition: a defensible timeline citing the relevant Windows artifacts.
