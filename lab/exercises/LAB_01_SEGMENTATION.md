# Lab 01 — Cisco Segmentation and Recovery Isolation

Scenario: the enterprise user network and server network are operational, but the recovery vault must remain isolated.

Student mission:
- Map the four VLANs from observed network information.
- Determine which zones require communication.
- Validate the isolation boundary around VAULT-01.
- Identify any simulated policy violation included by the instructor.
- Produce a short segmentation finding with evidence.

Success condition: the analyst can explain why compromise of USERS/SERVERS does not imply compromise of VAULT.
