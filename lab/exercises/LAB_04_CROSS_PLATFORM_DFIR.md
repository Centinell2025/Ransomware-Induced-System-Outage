# Lab 04 — Cross-Platform DFIR Correlation

Use Cisco/network telemetry, Windows logs, backup records, and the Kali analyst workstation.

Student mission:
- Construct a single chronological incident timeline.
- Separate normal traffic from incident-related activity.
- Identify evidence of data staging.
- Assess whether the evidence proves completed exfiltration.
- Explain the difference between an observed event, an analytical inference, and the extortion actor's claim.

Success condition: a concise incident report whose major conclusions are reproducible from the supplied evidence.
