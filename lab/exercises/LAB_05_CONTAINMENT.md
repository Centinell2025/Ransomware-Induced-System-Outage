# Lab 05 — Containment and Business Recovery

Scenario: critical file services are unavailable and the investigation has identified affected identities and systems.

Student mission:
- Propose containment priorities without destroying evidence.
- Identify accounts/sessions requiring defensive revocation.
- Define which systems remain quarantined.
- Establish a staged restoration order.
- Define validation checks before restored services return to the lab network.

Success condition: a recovery plan that preserves evidence, restores from a validated source, and maintains human approval for major recovery decisions.
